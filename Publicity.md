## Why Creator-Led Marketing Works Best for Dev Tools

* **Zero Marketing Budget Required:** Reaching thousands of targeted developers costs time, not capital.
* **High Trust Factor:** Devs respect technical founders who share architectural details, open tech stacks (Electron + SQLite + Ollama), and real product decisions.
* **Instant Direct Feedback:** You get raw, honest input from power users, helping you fix bugs and refine features before charging premium B2B rates.

## The Self-Driven Community Launch Playbook

To launch effectively without spending all day on social media, focus on **high-signal developer channels**:

### 1. Hacker News (`Show HN`)

* **The Strategy:** Hacker News loves local-first tools, SQLite, and offline/privacy-focused AI workflows.
* **The Format:** Keep the title simple and technical:
> *Show HN: DevFlow AI – Local-first desktop companion for SDLC with SQLite & Ollama*


* **The Post:** Write a honest top comment explaining *why* you built it (e.g., 4 months of work, frustrated with cloud LLM data leaks, wanted offline SDLC automation).

### 2. Reddit (Targeted Subreddits)

* **The Channels:**
* `r/LocalLLaMA` (Emphasize on-device execution, Ollama integration, and low-latency local workflows).
* `r/programming` & `r/sideproject` (Focus on full SDLC coverage and local desktop utility).
* `r/FlutterDev`, `r/Python`, `r/dotnet` (Focus on the Test Generator and multi-framework support).


* **Golden Rule:** Don't sell. Show a 15–30 second GIF or clip of a specific feature in action (like Smart Context SQL queries or instant screen-to-workflow generation).

### 3. "Build in Public" on X (Twitter) & LinkedIn

* **The Content:** Post short, silent screen recordings (15–30 seconds) showing real terminal execution, draw.io diagram syncing, or multi-repo git status views.
* **The Hook:** Focus on developer friction points: *"Tired of pasting code back and forth into web AI chats? Here's how local SQL context query routing works inside a desktop app..."*

### 4. Local Tech Communities (Bangalore & Beyond)

* Share demo videos or write brief posts in technical Discord/Slack groups (e.g., local AI builder channels, developer groups).
* Attend local developer meetups or demo days to give a 5-minute live screen share of your tool.
